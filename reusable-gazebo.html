The Reusable Gazebo

A 2025 McGill SustainaVision Competition Winner

Students were tasked to develop innovative solutions to improve McGill's sustainability by accomplishing the university's long term goals of achieving zero waste by 2035 and carbon neutrality by 2040, through new designs, community programs, infrastructure, or anything in between.

This project presents a fully disassemblable wooden gazebo designed to promote sustainability through the principles of Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle, with a strong emphasis on reuse. Constructed from responsibly sourced, recyclable materials, the gazebo provides a functional and eco-friendly outdoor space that can be efficiently stored away during colder months, reducing unnecessary wear and prolonging its lifespan. All 3D models were made in Rhino 3D and renders were made using Lumion.

Conceptual render of the Reusable Gazebo

A key feature of the design is its integration with multi-purpose benches, which not only serve as seating but also function as storage for the gazebo components when not in use. These benches can be repurposed for indoor use or conveniently stored, ensuring year-round usability and versatility. Additionally, the gazebo incorporates solar panels to provide renewable energy for phone charging, reinforcing its commitment to sustainable innovation.
